Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

ArheLover Marketing Pin Studio is a private tool operated by the owner of the ArheLover Pinterest account. It researches marketing topics and prepares draft content for that account.

Information the tool processes

  • Pinterest authorization tokens and account or board identifiers needed to connect the owner's account and publish Pins. Tokens are stored encrypted in the tool's local database.
  • Draft Pin titles, descriptions, alt text, generated images, selected boards, and source links used to prepare and review content.
  • Marketing research prompts and related content sent to OpenAI to provide research, writing, and image-generation features.
  • Information returned by Pinterest API endpoints that the account owner explicitly authorizes the tool to access.

How information is used

Information is used to research marketing topics, create and store drafts, connect to Pinterest, and publish an individual Pin only after the account owner reviews and approves that Pin. Research uses sources outside Pinterest; the tool does not scrape Pinterest or collect competitor data from Pinterest.

Service providers and sharing

OpenAI processes prompts and content needed for the AI features. Pinterest processes account data and Pins under its own terms and privacy policy. The operator does not sell personal information. Data is not shared with other parties except as needed to provide these features, comply with law, or protect the service.

Storage and deletion

The current version is intended for private, local use by one account owner. The owner can disconnect Pinterest access in Pinterest's account settings and delete locally stored data by removing it from the app's database. Disconnecting the app does not itself remove Pins already published to Pinterest.

Security

The local app encrypts stored Pinterest tokens. No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security; the operator should keep app credentials private and secure the device and database used to run the app.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the operator through the ArheLover Studio GitHub profile.
